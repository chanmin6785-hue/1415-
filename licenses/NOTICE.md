# Third-party data and assets

Application code is GPL-2.0-or-later; see GPL-2.0.txt for the license text. Source code and the data transformations are provided in this repository, without warranty.

- Historical league results: James P. Curley, engsoccerdata, GPL >= 2. https://github.com/jalapic/engsoccerdata . Normalized extracts are in `dist/replay/leagues/` for seasons before 2025. The extraction/filtering code is in `scripts/build_replay.py`.
- Recent league results (2025/26 and 2026/27): Football-Data.co.uk, https://www.football-data.co.uk/data.php . Attribution and any original usage conditions remain applicable. These files are not relicensed as application code.
- International results and goal events: Mart Jürisoo and contributors, CC0. https://github.com/martj42/international_results . See international-results-CC0.txt.
- World Cup data: OpenFootball and contributors, CC0. https://github.com/openfootball/worldcup.json . See openfootball-worldcup-CC0.txt.
- European club competition CSV: FootballCSV and contributors, CC0. https://github.com/footballcsv/europe-champions-league . See footballcsv-CC0.md.
- Recent European club competition TXT: OpenFootball and contributors, CC0. https://github.com/openfootball/champions-league .
- Editorial reports: brief original Korean summaries with source URLs in `dist/replay/stories.json`; original reporting remains the property of its respective publisher. Headlines in the app are written summaries, not purported verbatim quotations. Articles are not included in full.
- Stadium photograph: Unsplash photo identifier `photo-1522778119026-d647f0596c20`, used under the Unsplash license, https://unsplash.com/license . This is atmosphere imagery, not a photograph of any specified historical fixture.
- Web fonts: DM Sans and Noto Sans KR via Google Fonts. Font licenses are supplied by their respective upstream distributors. System fallback fonts work if remote font loading is unavailable.

Original factual data may contain errors. This application displays date-limited reconstructions and explicitly identifies limitations of standings, historical naming, and editorial coverage.

- K League 1/2 (2013 onward): Korean Professional Football League, public official schedule/result JSON at https://www.kleague.com/getScheduleList.do . Attribution retained; no private API credentials, full articles or copyrighted player photos redistributed by that schedule feed.
- Curated factual career year/date summaries: individual source links in `dist/replay/journeys.json`. They do not claim individual match appearances.

- Player profiles, dated transfers and appearances: D. Caribou and transfermarkt-datasets contributors, CC0-1.0, https://github.com/dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets ; public Kaggle mirror https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/davidcariboo/player-scores . Snapshot 2026-07-06, appearances through 2026-06-28. Selected, normalized extracts exclude profile-current clubs, current caps, valuations and player images. Player photos are not redistributed. See transfermarkt-datasets-CC0.txt.

- Korean player and club labels: Wikidata, CC0 structured data, matched by Transfermarkt IDs P2446 and P7223. https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing . Labels are display aliases; the canonical records remain unchanged.
- Country names, Korean translations and alpha-2 codes: mledoze/countries, Open Database License (ODbL), https://github.com/mledoze/countries . See countries-ODbL.txt. The included country-name/code extract is a derived database under ODbL.
- Korean club-name reference: https://github.com/dwoony0909-tech/korean-football-team-names . Factual display names retained with source attribution.
- Club/federation crests: current publicly served Transfermarkt images at https://tmssl.akamaized.net/images/wappen/head/{club_id}.png and SVGs from https://github.com/JoseArroyave/football-logos . Copyright/trademark rights remain with their respective clubs, federations and owners; these images are not relicensed under the application's GPL. Current crests are identified as current and are not verified historical designs. Source manifests can be regenerated by scripts/fetch_crests.py and scripts/extend_crest_assets.py.
- National flags: https://flagcdn.com/ (Flagpedia). Displayed as flags, distinct from federation badges. Current flags are not asserted as historical national designs.

- Dated football news link index: The Guardian's public daily football archives (1998–2026) and explicitly dated historical newspaper reprints. Newspaper article bodies and images are not included. Titles/translated titles and original source links remain attributed to their publishers and are not relicensed as GPL. The current archive title can reflect later edits.
- Korean editorial reports: short original Korean paraphrases with publication-date evidence and links in `data/editorial-sources.json` and `dist/replay/news/editorial.json`. They distinguish reported claims, the source's attributed reactions and the app's explanatory context. They are not verbatim articles or fabricated interviews. Sources include Sky Sports, UEFA, FC Barcelona, Tottenham Hotspur, The Irish Times, Irish Examiner and The Dong-A Ilbo, Yonhap News Agency and KBS WORLD.
- Historical records: Wikipedia contributors, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current_events and cited yearly football/season pages. Selected, normalized and where available translated excerpts are adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . These adapted records retain that license, distinct from application code. Each record links to its original page; contributor history is available there. Manual Korean historical case summaries are also shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Wikinews reporting: Wikinews contributors, https://en.wikinews.org/wiki/Category:Football_(soccer) . Text before 2005-09-25 is public domain; text from 2005-09-25 through 2024-12-15 is CC BY 2.5 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/); text from 2024-12-16 is CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Exact source URLs, dates and per-record licenses are retained. Where a translation exists it is identified as an adaptation; source images are not redistributed.
- FTR-18 football transfer news bibliography: Danielle Caled and Mário J. Silva, https://github.com/dcaled/FTR-18 ; metadata/software license MIT, see FTR-18-MIT.txt. Original news text remains with its publishers and is not redistributed. The bibliography supplies 2018 publication dates and public source URLs; inaccessible or conflicting sources are separately listed, not counted as verified news. No Twitter corpus, account metadata or future rumour-resolution labels are redistributed.
